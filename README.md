This is a Reqview Project on Volunteer Management System.

Objective:
After the COVID-19 pandemic, Kamloops Mayor’s Office has lost its connection with volunteers.
According to the city’s periodical crime report, volunteer activities can reduce the crime rate in the
city. Besides, community houses, senior people, and people with special needs suffer from a
shortage of available workers. Hence, the city mayor thought about designing a software system
(i.e., the Volunteer Management System) that connects the assistants with people. To encourage
participation, local businesses (e.g., local shops) will offer promotions and discounts for assistants
based on their participation. The Mayor’s Office needs an online system to enable volunteers to
register, approve volunteers, keep a record of their activities, and assign new assignments to
volunteers. 

Scope:
The project will involve understanding user requirements, creating detailed specifications, and
providing a solution that meets these requirements. The project report should outline the
functionality, performance, and constraints of a system or software product. The goal is to provide
a detailed description of how the software should behave, ensuring that both developers and
stakeholders have a clear understanding of the expectations.
